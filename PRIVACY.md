# Privacy Policy — betweenyour.thoughts tools

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

This policy covers the small tool that runs the Instagram account **@betweenyour.thoughts** (the "page"). The tool is run only by the page owner for the page owner's own account.

## What the tool does
- Publishes quote reels to @betweenyour.thoughts.
- Reads the page's own Instagram direct-message conversations and sends replies to people who messaged the page first, within Instagram's 24-hour messaging window.

## What data it uses
- The Instagram usernames and user IDs of people who have messaged the page, and the time of their latest message. This is used only to know who can be replied to.
- Message content is **not** stored, logged or shared.

## What we don't do
- We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone.
- We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.
- We do not message anyone who has not messaged the page first.

## Storage and retention
Nothing is stored after a run finishes. Data is fetched from Instagram when needed and discarded afterwards. The access token is kept as an encrypted secret.

## Your choices
You can stop all messages by blocking or restricting @betweenyour.thoughts on Instagram, or by asking us to delete any data: DM @betweenyour.thoughts. Deletion requests are handled within 7 days.

## Contact
Send a direct message to @betweenyour.thoughts on Instagram.
